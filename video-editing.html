Course Overview

What is Video Editing?

Video Editing is the art of assembling, refining and polishing raw footage into a compelling final video. It combines cutting, transitions, audio, color and motion to shape how a story is told — turning simple clips into content that captivates and connects with audiences.

Video is now the most powerful way brands, creators and businesses communicate online. From YouTube and Instagram Reels to ads and promotional films, every platform runs on great video. Learning video editing opens doors to freelancing, content creation and professional studio work — and this course teaches it all through practical, project-based training.
