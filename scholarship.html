Required Information

Information You May Be Asked For

When you request scholarship consideration, the admissions team may ask you to share some basic information to review your request.

  • Full Name Your full name as you would like it to appear on records.
  • Contact Information A phone number or email address where the team can reach you.
  • Selected Course The course you are interested in joining.
  • Educational Background Your highest level of education or relevant training.
  • Reason for Requesting Assistance A short explanation of why you are requesting support.
  • Relevant Academic/Professional Information Any details that support your request, if applicable.

Simple And Sensible

You will only be asked for basic information needed to review your request. Sensitive documents are never requested at this stage — they are only requested when necessary and only after you have been in contact with the admissions team.
