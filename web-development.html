Course Overview

What is Web & App Development?

Web and app development is the process of building websites and applications that people use every day — from business websites and online stores to interactive tools and mobile-friendly apps. It combines design, code and logic to create digital products that work beautifully across all devices.

These skills are among the most valuable in the world today. Every business needs a website, and every idea needs an app. Developers are in high demand everywhere — for jobs, freelancing and launching your own products. In this course you learn by doing, building real projects step by step from day one.
