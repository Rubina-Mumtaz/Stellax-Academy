Course Overview

What is Digital Marketing?

Digital Marketing is the promotion of products, services and brands using online channels such as search engines, social media, email and websites. It connects businesses with the right audience at the right time — and turns attention into real customers and sales.

Today, every business needs a strong online presence to survive and grow. Digital marketing skills are in high demand around the world, opening doors to careers, freelancing and entrepreneurship. Companies are actively hiring marketers who can plan campaigns, drive traffic and measure real results.
